Daily B2C Order Batching into Business Central

This demonstration shows a common integration pattern: 1 day of Shopify B2C orders becomes 1 consolidated Business Central sales order per channel, posted by a service identity, safe to rerun, with business logic kept in Business Central.

Demonstration with fictional data. No live system is connected. Store names, products, customers and orders are invented. The page simulates the flow logic and shows the exact Business Central API payload the production flow would send.
1Scheduled triggerPower Automate recurrence at 02:00 for the prior business day, running as an organization-owned service identity.
2Read ordersShopify Admin API, once per store, filtered to paid B2C orders for the batch date.
3Validate and mapEach SKU is checked against Business Central items. Unmapped lines go to an exception queue, not into the order.
4Rerun checkLook up an existing order by external document number STORE-YYYYMMDD. If found, skip.
5Create 1 order per channelPOST to the Business Central API v2.0 salesOrders endpoint with lines summed by item.
6Log and validateRun log and exceptions land where Power BI checks daily that source totals equal Business Central totals.

Design principles applied

Logic in the system that owns the dataPricing, tax, posting and inventory rules stay in Business Central. The flow only reads, sums and sends.
No database in the middleThe flow calls the Shopify and Business Central APIs directly. Nothing is staged in an intermediate database.
RerunnableThe external document number STORE-YYYYMMDD makes every run idempotent. A second run for the same day posts nothing.
Tied to no individualThe flow authenticates with a Microsoft Entra app registration the organization owns, registered in Business Central with only the permissions it needs.
Exceptions surfaced, not lostUnmapped SKUs are held back and logged, so the order posts clean and the gap is visible the same morning.
PortableThe orchestration is 6 plain steps over standard APIs, so it can move to another platform without rewriting business logic.
